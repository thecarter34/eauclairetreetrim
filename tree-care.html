Our Process

How We Work

  • Assessment

    Every health care engagement starts with a free on-site assessment. We'll look at the tree, discuss what you've observed, and explain what we're seeing.

  • Diagnosis

    We assess the tree's condition — looking at leaves, bark, structure, and soil. Many issues have visible signs; some require samples and lab analysis.

  • Treatment Plan

    Once we understand the problem, we create a treatment plan with realistic expectations. We explain what's likely to work, what will take time, and what the costs are.

  • Follow-Up

    Tree health is an ongoing relationship. We check progress, adjust treatments, and help you establish a care routine that keeps your trees healthy over the long term.

Common Questions

Tree Health Care FAQ

Answers to questions from Eau Claire homeowners about tree health and treatment.

How do I know if my tree needs health care?

Signs of trouble include unusual leaf discoloration, premature leaf drop, bark damage or peeling, dead branches in the crown, fungal growth on the trunk or roots, and visibly stressed foliage. If something looks off, call us for an assessment — early detection matters.

Can you treat emerald ash borer or other pest infestations?

We can help with pest identification and, in some cases, treatment options. Emerald ash borer is particularly challenging — preventive treatment is most effective before infestation. Call us to assess your ash trees and discuss your options.

What does tree fertilization cost?

Cost depends on tree size, number of trees, and the type of treatment needed. We provide a free assessment and give you an honest estimate before any work begins.

Is treatment always successful?

Not always. Some tree health problems are too advanced by the time they're discovered. We give you honest assessments and won't sell you treatments that are unlikely to help. When a tree can't be saved, we explain that too.
