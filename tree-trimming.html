Common Questions

Tree Trimming FAQ

Answers to questions Eau Claire homeowners ask before scheduling pruning work.

When is the best time to prune trees?

Most deciduous trees are best pruned during late winter or early spring when they're dormant — before new growth begins. Flowering trees should be pruned right after they bloom. We assess each tree individually and time the work accordingly.

How often should I have my trees trimmed?

Most residential trees benefit from pruning every 3-5 years. Fast-growing species may need attention more frequently. We'll assess your trees during the free assessment and give you a recommended schedule.

Can I trim my own trees?

Small ornamental trees and shrubs can sometimes be handled by homeowners. However, large tree pruning requires professional climbing equipment, proper training, and an understanding of tree biology. Improper cuts can harm the tree. When in doubt, call us.

What does proper pruning cost?

Cost depends on the number and size of trees, their condition, and the type of work needed. We provide a free on-site estimate so you know the cost before we start. There's no obligation.
