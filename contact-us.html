Common Questions

Questions Homeowners Ask

How quickly can you come out?

For most calls in Eau Claire, we can schedule same-day or next-day. Chippewa Falls and surrounding areas are typically within 2-3 days. Call (715) 579-1942 to check current availability.

Do you do free estimates?

Yes. Initial assessments are free. If the work requires an on-site assessment, we'll come out at no charge, walk your property, and give you a straightforward price estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We're fully licensed and carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation. Certificates are available on request.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, check, and Venmo. Payment is due upon completion of the work. We'll confirm payment options when we schedule the job.
