How quickly can you reach Chippewa Falls?

We're based in Eau Claire, about 10 miles south of Chippewa Falls. For most calls, we can schedule same-day or next-day appointments. Call (715) 579-1942 to arrange a time.

Do you handle lake properties around Chippewa Falls?

Yes. We work on properties around Lake Wissota, the Chippewa River corridor, and other waterfront areas. We have equipment suitable for tight access points common to lake properties and take care to protect shoreline landscaping.

What tree species are common in Chippewa Falls?

The Chippewa Valley has a mix of native hardwoods — oak, maple, birch, and aspen — plus ash (watching the emerald ash borer situation closely), cottonwoods along the river, and ornamental trees in established neighborhoods. We know the local species and their specific care needs.
